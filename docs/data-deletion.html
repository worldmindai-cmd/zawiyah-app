Data Deletion

Zawiyah has no account system and no server-side storage of your personal data — everything described in the Privacy Policy is stored locally on your device only. There is no account to delete and nothing on our servers to request removal of.

To remove specific data, without uninstalling

  • A Morning or Evening A'māl counter: open the item and use its reset control, or leave the routine to reset it automatically.
  • A private action or custom dua/a'māl request: remove it directly from the Actions list.
  • A downloaded reference text: open it in the Library and choose "Remove from device."

To remove all data

Uninstall the app. This permanently deletes the local database and all preferences described in the Privacy Policy — language setting, text size, devotional progress, private actions, and downloaded text ids. Nothing is retained after uninstall, because nothing was ever sent to us.

Questions

If you're unsure whether some data still exists after following the steps above, contact worldmindai@worldmindai.com.
