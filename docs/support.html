Content questions

Some sections of Zawiyah (such as certain biographies and kahanis) are clearly labelled as placeholder content pending sourcing and scholarly review. If you have a concern about the accuracy or appropriateness of any content that is not labelled as a placeholder, please write to us — we take this seriously and will review it.
