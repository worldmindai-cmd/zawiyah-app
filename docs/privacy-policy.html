Privacy Policy

Effective date: 18 July 2026

Zawiyah is developed by WorldMind AI ("we," "us"). This policy explains what information Zawiyah collects, how it's used, and how it's stored. Zawiyah is built to minimise data collection — most of what the app does happens entirely on your device.

No account, no sign-in

Zawiyah does not require you to create an account or sign in. There is no login, and we do not collect your name, email address, or any identifying information to use the app.

What is stored, and where

The following information is stored locally on your device only, and is never transmitted to us or to any third party:

  • Your selected role (e.g. "Daily worshipper," "Parent / Family") from onboarding
  • Your selected language preference
  • Progress on devotional counters (e.g. tasbeeh, Morning A'māl)
  • Personal Collections you create, and their contents
  • Notification preferences (which reminder categories, if any, you've turned on)
  • A manual Hijri date adjustment, if you've set one

This data lives in a local database on your device. We have no access to it, cannot see it, and cannot recover it if your device is lost — there is no cloud backup or sync in this version of the app.

No advertising, no selling data

Zawiyah contains no advertising and no advertising SDKs. We do not sell, rent, or share your data with third parties, because we do not collect any data that leaves your device in the first place.

No analytics or tracking

This version of Zawiyah does not include analytics or tracking software. We do not know how you use the app, how often you open it, or what content you view.

Text-to-speech

Zawiyah's read-aloud feature (used for Kahani) uses your device's built-in voice engine (e.g. the system voices on iOS or Android). Audio is generated and played entirely on your device; the text being read is not sent to us or to any third-party voice service.

Notifications

Notification categories are off by default. If you turn one on, reminders are scheduled locally on your device — no notification data is sent through an external push notification service, and no server is involved.

Children's privacy

Zawiyah offers a "Child / Young Zawiyah" role intended for use by children with a parent or guardian. Choosing this role does not collect any information about a child — it only stores the role selection itself, locally, the same as any other role. Zawiyah does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children, and contains no advertising or in-app purchases.

Deleting your data

Uninstalling Zawiyah permanently deletes all locally-stored data described above. Within the app, a "Reset counters" option is also available if you want to clear devotional progress without uninstalling.

Future features

Any future feature that would send data off your device — such as optional on-demand content downloads, or AI-assisted study tools that call an external service — will only activate with your explicit consent, and this policy will be updated first to describe exactly what is sent and why.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the effective date above will be updated. Material changes will be reflected here before they take effect.

Contact

Questions about this policy or your data can be sent to worldmindai@worldmindai.com.
