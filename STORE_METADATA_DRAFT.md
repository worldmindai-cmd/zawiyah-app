## Full description
Zawiyah is a calm, privacy-conscious spiritual companion created to help users draw closer to Allah through the Holy Qur'an and the teachings of the Ahlul Bayt (AS).

Version 1 includes a suggested morning a'mal collection with Arabic, transliteration and complete English meaning; persistent haptic counters; full-screen tap-anywhere counting for sajdah and eyes-closed recitation; a device-date-based Shia calendar; automatic mourning presentation; a digital tasbeeh; and a devotional kahani reader using the phone's text-to-speech voice.

Zawiyah does not replace scholars or maraji'. Its purpose is to organise, explain and support worship. Canonical religious content must remain traceable to its source, and AI must never invent narrations or issue fatwas.

No advertising is included.
