# Zawiyah Vision

> **Truth before convenience.**

Zawiyah exists to help people seek closeness to Allah (SWT) through the Holy Qur'an and the teachings of the Ahlul Bayt (AS), using technology responsibly, transparently and with humility.

Every design decision should increase trust, reduce complexity and help people worship, learn and grow.

## Founding principles

- Authenticity before AI.
- Reduce confusion, not the religion.
- AI assists; it does not replace scholars or marājiʿ.
- Provenance must be visible.
- User attention and personal data are an amānah.
- Worship should not depend unnecessarily on connectivity.
- The Constitution takes priority over engineering convenience.
