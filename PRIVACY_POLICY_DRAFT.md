# Zawiyah Privacy Policy — Draft

Zawiyah is designed to minimise data collection. Version 1 stores onboarding role selection, devotional counter progress and personal settings locally on the user's device. Zawiyah v1 contains no advertising and does not sell personal data.

Text-to-speech uses the voice services available on the user's device. Any future WorldMind AI or cross-application context feature will require explicit consent and a separate, updated privacy notice before activation.

Users can remove local app data by deleting the application. A future release may provide more granular data deletion controls.

Contact and legal entity details must be inserted before publication.
